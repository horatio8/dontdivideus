The pledge

We demand Victoria's leaders repeal the Statewide Treaty Act 2025.

This Act is a profound betrayal of the principles of equality and democracy. It lacks a clear public mandate, and its cost will burden all Victorians.

We reject any legislation that divides our community by creating a two-tiered society — and we commit our votes, our voices and our networks to restoring one Victoria, where every citizen stands equal before the law.
