Our purpose

Don't Divide Us is a Victorian campaign for the repeal of the Statewide Treaty Act 2025. Our stance is pro-unity, pro-equality and pro-democracy: we believe disadvantage demands serious policy delivered under equal laws — not parallel civic institutions defined by ancestry. We make our case from the text of the Act, publish the government's responses alongside our own, and fact-check ourselves in public.

Funding posture

We accept donations only from Australian citizens, residents and Australian-registered entities, within Victorian caps. Money is spent on advertising, organising staff, printed material and this website. It is not spent on payments to spokespeople for their views.
