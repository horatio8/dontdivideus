Timeline

  • Jan 2024

    Coalition withdraws from the treaty process

    The Victorian Liberals and Nationals end bipartisan support for treaty negotiations, citing the referendum result.

  • 9 Sep 2025

    Statewide Treaty Bill introduced

    Premier introduces the Bill to the Legislative Assembly.

  • 16 Oct 2025

    Coalition votes against in the Assembly

    The Bill passes after a 13-hour overnight sitting; every Coalition MP votes No. Vote recorded per member on Find your MP.

  • 30 Oct 2025

    Coalition votes against in the Council; Bill passes

    The Bill clears the upper house with crossbench support. Repeal commitment follows.

  • 1 May 2026

    Gellung Warl established

    The Act's central body begins operating with statutory powers.
