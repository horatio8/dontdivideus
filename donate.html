Compliance, stated plainly

Donations are not tax deductible. Donations may only be accepted from Australian citizens or residents and Australian-registered entities. Disclosure thresholds and caps under the Electoral Act 2002 (Vic) apply, and our donations are reported to the VEC as required. Read the full donations policy.
