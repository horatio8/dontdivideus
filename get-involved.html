The theory of change

How this actually wins

No mystery, no hashtags. Four steps, repeated in twelve seats until November.

  1. Sign

    A pledge with a postcode becomes pressure in a specific seat — visible to its MP.

  2. Share

    Two Victorias cards travel further than any ad budget. One share reaches the persuadable.

  3. Recruit

    Each pledger who brings three friends doubles the list every month it matters.

  4. Vote

    November 28. The election Victorians were never given on this Act.
